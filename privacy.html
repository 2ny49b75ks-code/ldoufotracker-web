Privacy Policy

LDO — UFO Detector app, published by JDG

Last updated: August 7, 2026
In short: LDO analyzes your captures directly on your iPhone. No video, photo, or audio you record is ever sent to a server — there is no server. The only personal data processed is your location, used solely to place your captures on a map inside the app.

01What data LDO uses

LDO requests the following permissions on your device, each used only for the function described:

DataWhy
CameraFilm the observed object and enable image analysis (trajectory, shape, illumination).
MicrophoneAnalyze the recording's audio track to recognize sound signatures (aircraft, helicopter, bird, insect).
Location (GPS, only while using the app)Place your capture on a map in the results screen. Requested once at recording time, never in the background.
Photo LibrarySave your captures to Photos, and let you import an existing video from your library to analyze.

Each permission is requested by iOS only when it becomes necessary, and you can decline it — some LDO features will then simply be unavailable or shown as "not calculable," rather than blocking the app.

02Where your data goes

  • Analysis (trajectory, speed, G-forces, distance, illumination, sound, verdict) happens entirely on your iPhone. No video, photo, or audio clip is ever transmitted to JDG or to any third party.
  • LDO has no server, no user account, and uses no analytics or advertising-tracking tools.
  • Your videos and photos stay in your iCloud Photo Library (per your usual iOS settings) and in the app's local storage, just like any other camera app.
  • Your location is never linked to your identity or shared — it's used only to show a point on the map in a capture's results, on your own device.

03Tracking and advertising

LDO does not track your activity, whether inside the app or across other apps and websites. There is no advertising in LDO.

04Your choices

  • You can revoke access to the camera, microphone, location, or photo library at any time in iOS Settings → LDO.
  • You can delete your recordings individually, either in the app or in Photos.
  • Deleting the app erases all data LDO kept locally on your device (except copies already saved to your Photo Library, which follow your usual Photos/iCloud settings).

05Children

LDO is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

06Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

07Contact us

For any question about this policy or your data: ldoufotracker@outlook.com
